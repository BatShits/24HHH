# Working on this repo

Offline PWA for 24 Hours of Horseshoe Hell (Zack and his partner). Served by GitHub Pages from `/docs`. No build tooling or dependencies: plain HTML/CSS/JS.

- After changing anything in `docs/` or `data-src/`, run `python3 tools/build_data.py`. It regenerates `docs/data/*.json`, and stamps a new version into `docs/sw.js` and `docs/version.js` so phones pick up the update.
- Keep it working offline: every new file the app needs must be added to `SHELL` in `docs/sw.template.js` (edit the template, never `docs/sw.js`).
- Map tiles: USGS National Map, native max zoom 16; the app overzooms to 19. Offline region is `BBOX` in `docs/map.js`.
- Notes live in each phone's localStorage (`hhh.notes`). Exports are JSON files `hhh-notes-<climber>-<date>.json`; merge them into `data-src/` when Zack sends one.
- Don't commit verbatim Mountain Project comment text (the repo is public); keep derived numbers and short area notes only.
- Times shown to users use 12-hour format (1:30 pm). Filenames use ISO dates.
- Planner (`docs/plan.js`): recommends a plan from format (12/24), one or more goals `plan.goals` (laps / points / feet / trad-lap targets and/or an achievement; all must be met, `goalsOf`; numeric goals add value per route, a met one counts 0.2x; a coverage goal drives the zone tour; `plan.goal` = first goal for old code; new plans start with no goals and the recommend button stays disabled until one is picked), side goals, per-climber division (defaults from profile project grade), intensity (Don't Hurt Me / Bring it On! / I am Death Incarnate!!) and options; one counted lap per route per climber. Path first: `build()` tries four path shapes (`PATH_MODES`: loop round the top W-first / E-first, or a U through the valley crossing at the south end, E-first / W-first) and keeps the one that meets the goals at the gentlest pace with least walking (`plan.pathMode`; start walls skip this). The optimizer then moves forward only along that path, choosing among the next `PATH_WINDOW` (2) walls that still have something to climb; turning back is allowed only when nothing ahead fits (12-hour once, 24-hour twice, plus a last-resort pass before idling); valley walls sit off the path; coverage tours follow path order and don't linger at a finished wall past its share of the time left. Wall-level rolling-horizon optimizer (45 min look-ahead, walking weighted 9x (`WALKW`), plus `TUNE` penalties for any move, returning to a wall, reversing the sweep direction along the cliff line (`linePos`, angle round the horseshoe) and valley detours). Canyon crossings (West+North vs East) capped by `MAX_CROSS` (12-hour 1, 24-hour 2) with `CROSS_PEN` making the second one costly so one is preferred; coverage goals first build a zone tour, one side then the other (nearest neighbour + 2-opt per side), and follow it strictly in order. After the search, `polish()` folds revisits into the first visit and moves whole wall stops when that cuts walking and `keepsRules()` still passes (time-dependent grade caps, dark limit, warm-up ramp, hard laps per hour, crossings, event end, goals). No user-specific pace: `build()` binary-searches a lead-time multiplier (paceF) for the slowest steady pace that still meets every goal a contingency buffer before the end (`BUFFER`: Full Horseshoe/qualify 4 h on the 24, 2 h on the 12; other goals 2 h / 1 h; falls back to half, then no buffer via `plan.bufF`; `goalTimes()` replays the plan to find when each goal is met; the summary table's first row is "Goals met by" with the buffer, the plan list draws a green line after the climb that completes the goals, and the summary warns when short of the aim); the UI shows required pace as target laps / event hours (laps per hour per climber). Walking: trail distance (`areas.json` `d`, built by `tools/trails.py` from `data-src/trails_osm.json`, OpenStreetMap ODbL, keep the attribution) at a push-level speed (50 / 90 / 140 m/min: stroll, fast walk, jog) plus 2 / 1 / 0.5 min; straight line x1.3 only as a fallback. Map plan legs follow the trail graph in `docs/data/trails.json`. Check-ins: the plan walks to `CHECKIN_LOC` (one Trading Post point until the four station locations are known) from wherever it is when the window opens, 10 min there, then walks on. Breaks: `plan.breakMin` minutes once an hour, defaulting from push level (15 / 5 / 0). Event hours are fixed by format (12-hour 7:30 am to 7:30 pm, 24-hour 10:00 am to 10:00 am); `plan.start` is forced from `FORMATS` on load and import. Sweep limited to `TUNE.turns` turnarounds per side (relaxed only if nothing else fits); no second crossing in the last 3 hours. Sun preference `plan.sunPref` (shade: only the East side is penalized, from noon while light, by its sun state, since West and North walls have tree cover; sun: favor sunny walls in daylight; none: no sun effect; legacy `avoidSun:false` = none). Start walls `plan.startWalls` (ordered; fresh builds only): at least ~45 min (H) at each, then move to the next once it beats staying; unclimbable ones are skipped. Option `plan.together` (default on, "Same routes for both"): strict pass only picks routes both climbers can lead. Plan list: drag handles (pointer events) reorder a route within its own wall only, or move a wall stop with all its routes; summary shows a routes-by-grade bar chart beside the table, and team rows Efficiency (A 92%+ / B 88 / C 84 / D 80 / F, share of walking+climbing time spent climbing) and Walk : climb. Wall stops collapse (▸/▾ per stop, keyed wall#nth visit in `collapsedStops`; "Collapse all walls" / "Expand all"); collapsed headers show the route count. Wall headers have "+ Add" (picker filtered to that wall, inserts at the end of that stop); grade limits `plan.gradeMax` {grade label: max per climber} are set under the chart and applied on rebuild. Sport grade range `plan.gmin`/`plan.gmax` and trad grade range `plan.tmin`/`plan.tradMax` (each applies only to its type; mixed counts as sport) and warm-up `plan.warm` + `plan.warmN` (default 3; routes per climber at or below the warm-up grade first, never below gmin), compared by grade bucket (`gradeLabel`/`GU2`). Map has a play button (`#play`) that steps the clock through the active plan in 15-minute steps. Routes Mountain Project rates stiff or leans stiff (`r.v`) are never planned at or above a climber's onsight grade. Hardest grade after dark `plan.darkMax` (grade label; anything harder is skipped when light is not day and weighted 1.5x in daylight). Warm-up then ramps one grade step every two routes. Without a score goal, route value leans on mid/lower grades (x1.2 for 1-4 steps under onsight, 0.8 at onsight, 0.5 above). When nothing fits, the optimizer adds one growing idle item (`idle: true`, IDLE_LABEL) and the summary warns how long and from when. Manual plans (`plan.manual`, "Build it myself") use `MANUAL_PACE` by push level instead of paceF; wall picker stays open for several adds. 24-hour option `plan.earlyHard` (routes at or above onsight only in the first 12 hours). Routes tab groups by wall (walk order) with a quick grade/hide-above-project bar synced to the filter panel. Map: legend (`#mapLegend`), short plan banner at the top, drag arrows on the clock dial.
- Zone map (`data-src/aspects.py` ZONE_OF): the 24 zones are the 24 red-labelled walls on "The Full Horseshoe" map, last page of the 2024 rules PDF (The Park, Carrion Cube and Goat Cave are not zones). North Forty is split into four sub-areas by comp zone (`NF_SPLIT`, estimated positions). End routes (`SPECIALS`, route field `sp` = 'W'/'E'): Full Horseshoe needs one lap on any west-end route (#10, 14, 24, 33, 36) and any east-end route (#641, 642, 648, 650, 651).
