# Working on this repo

Offline PWA for 24 Hours of Horseshoe Hell (Zack and his partner). Served by GitHub Pages from `/docs`. No build tooling or dependencies: plain HTML/CSS/JS.

- After changing anything in `docs/` or `data-src/`, run `python3 tools/build_data.py`. It regenerates `docs/data/*.json`, and stamps a new version into `docs/sw.js` and `docs/version.js` so phones pick up the update.
- Keep it working offline: every new file the app needs must be added to `SHELL` in `docs/sw.template.js` (edit the template, never `docs/sw.js`).
- Map tiles: USGS National Map, native max zoom 16; the app overzooms to 19. Offline region is `BBOX` in `docs/map.js`.
- Notes live in each phone's localStorage (`hhh.notes`). Exports are JSON files `hhh-notes-<climber>-<date>.json`; merge them into `data-src/` when Zack sends one.
- Don't commit verbatim Mountain Project comment text (the repo is public); keep derived numbers and short area notes only.
- Times shown to users use 12-hour format (1:30 pm). Filenames use ISO dates.
- Planner (`docs/plan.js`): recommends a plan from format (12/24), one or more goals `plan.goals` (laps / points / feet / trad-lap targets and/or an achievement; all must be met, `goalsOf`; numeric goals add value per route, a met one counts 0.2x; a coverage goal drives the zone tour; `plan.goal` = first goal for old code; new plans start with no goals and the recommend button stays disabled until one is picked), side goals, per-climber division (defaults from profile project grade), intensity (Don't Hurt Me / Bring it On! / I am Death Incarnate!!) and options; one counted lap per route per climber. Path first: `build()` tries four path shapes (`PATH_MODES`: loop round the top W-first / E-first, or a U through the valley crossing at the south end, E-first / W-first) and keeps the one that meets the goals at the gentlest pace with least walking (`plan.pathMode`; start walls skip this). The optimizer then moves forward only along that path, choosing among the next `PATH_WINDOW` (2) walls that still have something to climb; turning back is allowed only when nothing ahead fits (12-hour once, 24-hour twice, plus a last-resort pass before idling); valley walls sit off the path, and the valley-floor crags The Park and The Carrion Cube are left out of planning entirely (`NO_PLAN_AREAS`, still listed in the Routes tab); coverage tours follow path order and don't linger at a finished wall past its share of the time left. Wall-level rolling-horizon optimizer (45 min look-ahead, walking weighted 9x (`WALKW`), plus `TUNE` penalties for any move, returning to a wall, reversing the sweep direction along the cliff line (`linePos`, angle round the horseshoe) and valley detours). Canyon crossings (West+North vs East) capped by `MAX_CROSS` (12-hour 1, 24-hour 2) with `CROSS_PEN` making the second one costly so one is preferred; coverage goals first build a zone tour, one side then the other (nearest neighbour + 2-opt per side), and follow it strictly in order. After the search, `polish()` folds revisits into the first visit and moves whole wall stops when that cuts walking and `keepsRules()` still passes (time-dependent grade caps, dark limit, warm-up ramp, hard laps per hour, crossings, event end, goals). No user-specific pace: `build()` binary-searches a lead-time multiplier (paceF) for the slowest steady pace that still meets every goal a contingency buffer before the end (`BUFFER`: Full Horseshoe/qualify 4 h on the 24, 2 h on the 12; other goals 2 h / 1 h; falls back to half, then no buffer via `plan.bufF`; `goalTimes()` replays the plan to find when each goal is met; the summary table's first row is "Goals met by" with the buffer, the plan list draws a green line after the climb that completes the goals, and the summary warns when short of the aim); the UI shows required pace as target laps / event hours (laps per hour per climber). Walking: trail distance (`areas.json` `d`, built by `tools/trails.py` from `data-src/trails_osm.json`, OpenStreetMap ODbL, keep the attribution) at a push-level speed (50 / 90 / 140 m/min: stroll, fast walk, jog) plus 2 / 1 / 0.5 min; straight line x1.3 only as a fallback. Map plan legs follow the trail graph in `docs/data/trails.json`. Check-ins: the plan walks to `CHECKIN_LOC` (one Trading Post point until the four station locations are known) from wherever it is when the window opens, 10 min there, then walks on. Breaks: `plan.breakMin` minutes once an hour, defaulting from push level (15 / 5 / 0). Event hours are fixed by format (12-hour 7:30 am to 7:30 pm, 24-hour 10:00 am to 10:00 am); `plan.start` is forced from `FORMATS` on load and import. Sweep limited to `TUNE.turns` turnarounds per side (relaxed only if nothing else fits); no second crossing in the last 3 hours. Sun preference `plan.sunPref` (shade: only the East side is penalized, from noon while light, by its sun state, since West and North walls have tree cover; sun: favor sunny walls in daylight; none: no sun effect; legacy `avoidSun:false` = none). Start walls `plan.startWalls` (ordered; fresh builds only): at least ~45 min (H) at each, then move to the next once it beats staying; unclimbable ones are skipped. Option `plan.together` (default on, "Same routes for both"): strict pass only picks routes both climbers can lead. Plan list: drag handles (pointer events) reorder a route within its own wall only, or move a wall stop with all its routes; summary shows a routes-by-grade bar chart beside the table, and team rows Efficiency (A 92%+ / B 88 / C 84 / D 80 / F, share of walking+climbing time spent climbing) and Walk : climb. Plan rows are numbered #1, #2… in climb order (`.p-num`). Wall stops collapse (▸/▾ per stop, keyed wall#nth visit in `collapsedStops`; "Collapse all walls" / "Expand all"); collapsed headers show the route count. Wall headers have "+ Add" (picker filtered to that wall, inserts at the end of that stop); grade limits `plan.gradeMax` {grade label: max per climber} are set under the chart and applied on rebuild. Sport grade range `plan.gmin`/`plan.gmax` and trad grade range `plan.tmin`/`plan.tradMax` (each applies only to its type; mixed counts as sport; route type `plan.routeType` (undefined = sport + trad, 'sport', 'trad'; legacy `plan.noTrad` = sport; `routeTypeOf`) and side of the canyon `plan.sideOnly` ('E' / 'W', West includes the North Forty) both filter the optimizer pool and the manual picker via `planFits`; the Goals section warns when a goal clashes (trad goal with sport only, Full/Golden/qualify with one side). East only turns off core-first) and warm-up `plan.warm` + `plan.warmN` (default 3; routes per climber at or below the warm-up grade first, never below gmin), compared by grade bucket (`gradeLabel`/`GU2`). Routing `plan.routing`: undefined = Auto (`build()` runs `buildWith` for 'simple' and 'flexible', keeps reachable → more buffer → 5%+ gentler paceF → less walking; `plan.routeUsed` records the pick and drives later re-plans via `routingOf`); 'simple' = path-first (`PATH_MODES` loopW/loopE/uE/uW, forward only, `PATH_WINDOW` walls ahead, limited flips); 'flexible' = older sweep rules (`TUNE.turns`, reverse penalty). Simple can miss goals or buffer on the 12-hour with conservative push or tight grade caps. Fatigue (`slowF`, `plan.fatigue`, default on, "Slow down at night"): lead time x (1 + `FATIGUE`[fmt] per hour since start: 24h 0.01, 12h 0.012) x `DARK_SLOW` 1.05 in the dark, both scaled by push level (`SLOW_SCALE`: Death Incarnate 0.75, Bring it On! 1 = the learned case, Don't Hurt Me 2). Push levels also set changeover per route (`CHANGEOVER` 3 / 1.5 / 1 min), walking (70 / 90 / 140 m/min) and breaks (10 / 5 / 0 min per hour). Don't Hurt Me and Bring it On! are never sped up to meet goals: `build()` uses a fixed paceF (`FIXED_PACE`: Bring it On! 0.6 on both = top 2026 Intermediates, about 114 / 69 laps for a 5.10a team; Don't Hurt Me 24h 0.75 / 12h 0.65 = just qualifies, about 74 / 41) and, when goals aren't met, keeps the plan and the summary says what it reaches. Only I am Death Incarnate!! searches, on one effort knob x (`setEffort`; `plan.effortX`, `plan.effort`): x -1..0 = Bring it On!'s settings with an eased climbing pace (paceF up to 3), x 0..1 = breaks, changeover, walking speed/prep and slowdown scale interpolate Bring it On! -> all-out (`push(plan)` returns the effective settings; use it, not INTENSITY/CHANGEOVER/SLOW_SCALE directly) while paceF goes 0.6 -> 0.3; it binary-searches the least x that meets the goals with the buffer, so a lower target means less effort with about the same buffer. A user-set break length overrides the scaling. Summary has a Pacing table (`pacingTable`, all push levels): effort (DI), 60 ft cruiser lead time fresh and at 3 am, per-route average, walking, breaks, slowdown (no multiplier or changeover rows: Zack finds them unhelpful). DI search is calibrated to timed logs of 26 top 2025-26 climbers (24h laps per 6-hour block about 26/28/24/21%; an early 3-climber sample that looked half-pace overnight did not hold up). End-route lines (`queueMin`, `plan.lineMin`, default 0: Zack wants the goal buffer, not a built-in wait, to cover lines) on the 5.8-and-easier bookends (Hickadelic Jazzgrass, Montezuma's), a sixth of that on harder ones; shown as a "Line ~N min" tag; the optimizer counts only 30% of it for a needed end route. `plan.coreFirst` (default on, "Start in the North Forty core", off for Full/Golden and start walls, `coreOn`): the first wall must be a North wall (except the last-resort pass), simple routing tries only `CORE_MODES` (coreW: North core → down the West → back up and cross East at the top; uW), and non-North walls score x0.6 for the first `CORE_H` hours (24h 6, 12h 4). Route softness: "Prefer soft-for-grade" (`plan.side.soft`, default on) x1.15, stiff x0.85, stiff never at or above onsight. Map has a play button (`#play`) that steps the clock through the active plan in 15-minute steps. Routes Mountain Project rates stiff or leans stiff (`r.v`) are never planned at or above a climber's onsight grade. Hardest grade after dark `plan.darkMax` (grade label; anything harder is skipped when light is not day and weighted 1.5x in daylight). Warm-up then ramps one grade step every two routes. Without a score goal, route value leans on mid/lower grades (x1.2 for 1-4 steps under onsight, 0.8 at onsight, 0.5 above). When nothing fits, the optimizer adds one growing idle item (`idle: true`, IDLE_LABEL) and the summary warns how long and from when. Manual plans (`plan.manual`, "Build it myself") use `MANUAL_PACE` by push level instead of paceF; wall picker stays open for several adds. 24-hour option `plan.earlyHard` (routes at or above onsight only in the first 12 hours). Routes tab groups by wall (walk order; wall headers fold, `ui.foldWalls`, plus "Collapse all walls"/"Expand all walls" `#foldAll`) with a quick grade/hide-above-project bar synced to the filter panel. Map: legend (`#mapLegend`), short plan banner at the top, drag arrows on the clock dial.
- Planning setup panel (`renderSetup`): collapsible sections (`.psec`, open state in `secOpen`, each header shows a one-line summary): Event, Goals, Push and pace, Climbers, Routes, Where, then the build buttons. Single choices use segmented controls (`segRow`), on/off settings use switches (`toggle`), multi-select goals stay as chips. Keep new settings in the matching section and in that style.
- Demon (`docs/demon.js`, `Demon.svg`/`Demon.splash`): the app's character, an angry red demon with a cracked horn and a headlamp. Loaded first in `<body>`; the opening screen (`#splash`, CSS `sp-*` keyframes, 5 s, tap to skip, calm static version under reduced motion) flickers the headlamp on, lights the eyes and snaps the mouth open then shut. Home-screen icons in `docs/icons/` are the open-mouth version. Zack is workshopping the demon's taunting personality (negativity as motivation, text only, no sound) outside the app; don't add taunts to the app until he says so.
- Tabs, left to right: Go Time! (`go`), Navigation (`map`), Planning (`plan`), Routes (`routes`), Climber Setup (`me`, gear icon, aria-label "Climber Setup"); data-tab keys kept from the old names. Go Time (`renderGo`/`liveStats` in plan.js) tracks the active plan against real Chicago time (`nowAbs`), hides the planning clock, refreshes every 30 s: minutes ahead/behind = min(planned end of the k-th route minus when the k-th tick happened, planned end of route k+1 minus now), green/gray/red, with signed lines under it: routes vs planned-by-now, routes/hour vs plan, projected finish vs plan (tiles coloured to match); routes per hour since start, last hour, projection, next route and check-in, per-climber totals vs goal targets.
- Plan sharing (`docs/share.js`, `PlanShare`): plan + ticks + both profiles packed (routes as [num, who 0/1/2, done minutes]), deflate-raw + base64url, link `#p=<code>`; Share shows a QR (vendored `vendor/qrcode.js`, MIT), "Scan a plan" reads it with BarcodeDetector or vendored `vendor/jsQR.js` (Apache-2.0), or a pasted link; import asks which climber you are and swaps me/partner (`importShared` in plan.js). A `#p=` link opened in a browser imports too (iPhone home-screen apps have separate storage, so scan from inside the app).
- Wall photos (`docs/photos.js`, `WallPhotos`): camera button `#snap` on Navigation (also "Add photo" in a wall's sheet) opens an in-app camera (getUserMedia + shutter; <input capture> hands off to the camera app and loses the photo in home-screen apps) with a "Photo library" fallback; GPS picks the nearest wall within 400 m (else pick it); photo shrunk to 1600 px JPEG and stored in IndexedDB `hhh-photos` with marks [{rid, x, y}] as fractions; editor lists the wall's routes by number, tap to place, drag to move, 1-3x zoom. Shown in the wall sheet and route detail ("Where it starts") as fixed-height thumbnails (width from aspect ratio); tapping opens a full-screen viewer (`view(list, i)`) that swipes between that set (scroll-snap carousel, locked while zoomed) and zooms 1-6x by pinch, double-tap or −/Fit/+; marks orange = in plan, green = done. Delete from the viewer (tap twice) or from the Wall photos list in Climber Setup (`manage`, per photo or all). Each record also keeps a 480 px `thumb` (made at save, or lazily by `ensureThumb` for older ones) used in rows and lists; the viewer loads full images only for the current photo ±1. Saving calls `navigator.storage.persist()`; Climber Setup shows count, size and quota. Photos never go in the repo; the notes export carries them as data URLs only when "Include wall photos" (`#expPhotos`, off by default) is ticked.
- `tools/build_data.py` runs `node --check` on the app JS (a syntax error in sw.js once silently stopped updates).
- Zone map (`data-src/aspects.py` ZONE_OF): the 24 zones are the 24 red-labelled walls on "The Full Horseshoe" map, last page of the 2024 rules PDF (The Park, Carrion Cube and Goat Cave are not zones). North Forty is split into four sub-areas by comp zone (`NF_SPLIT`, estimated positions). End routes (`SPECIALS`, route field `sp` = 'W'/'E'): Full Horseshoe needs one lap on any west-end route (#10, 14, 24, 33, 36) and any east-end route (#641, 642, 648, 650, 651).
