# Working on this repo

Offline PWA for 24 Hours of Horseshoe Hell (Zack and his partner). Served by GitHub Pages from `/docs`. No build tooling or dependencies: plain HTML/CSS/JS.

- After changing anything in `docs/` or `data-src/`, run `python3 tools/build_data.py`. It regenerates `docs/data/*.json`, and stamps a new version into `docs/sw.js` and `docs/version.js` so phones pick up the update.
- Keep it working offline: every new file the app needs must be added to `SHELL` in `docs/sw.template.js` (edit the template, never `docs/sw.js`).
- Map tiles: USGS National Map, native max zoom 16; the app overzooms to 19. Offline region is `BBOX` in `docs/map.js`.
- Notes live in each phone's localStorage (`hhh.notes`). Exports are JSON files `hhh-notes-<climber>-<date>.json`; merge them into `data-src/` when Zack sends one.
- Don't commit verbatim Mountain Project comment text (the repo is public); keep derived numbers and short area notes only.
- Times shown to users use 12-hour format (1:30 pm). Filenames use ISO dates.
- Planner (`docs/plan.js`): recommends a plan from format (12/24), goal with target (laps / points / feet, or an achievement), side goals, per-climber division (defaults from profile project grade), intensity (Don't Hurt Me / Bring it On! / I am Death Incarnate!!) and options; one counted lap per route per climber. Wall-level rolling-horizon optimizer (45 min look-ahead, walking weighted 2.5x, plus `TUNE` penalties for any move, returning to a wall, reversing the sweep direction along the cliff line (`linePos`, angle round the horseshoe) and valley detours). Canyon crossings (West+North vs East) capped by `MAX_CROSS` (12-hour 1, 24-hour 2) with `CROSS_PEN` making the second one costly so one is preferred; coverage goals first build a zone tour, one side then the other (nearest neighbour + 2-opt per side), and follow it strictly in order. No user-specific pace: `build()` binary-searches a lead-time multiplier (paceF) for the slowest steady pace that still meets the target; the UI shows required pace as target laps / event hours (laps per hour per climber). Walking: trail distance (`areas.json` `d`, built by `tools/trails.py` from `data-src/trails_osm.json`, OpenStreetMap ODbL, keep the attribution) at a push-level speed (50 / 90 / 140 m/min: stroll, fast walk, jog) plus 2 / 1 / 0.5 min; straight line x1.3 only as a fallback. Map plan legs follow the trail graph in `docs/data/trails.json`. Check-ins: the plan walks to `CHECKIN_LOC` (one Trading Post point until the four station locations are known) from wherever it is when the window opens, 10 min there, then walks on. Breaks: `plan.breakMin` minutes once an hour, defaulting from push level (15 / 5 / 0). Event hours are fixed by format (12-hour 7:30 am to 7:30 pm, 24-hour 10:00 am to 10:00 am); `plan.start` is forced from `FORMATS` on load and import. Sweep limited to `TUNE.turns` turnarounds per side (relaxed only if nothing else fits); no second crossing in the last 3 hours. Map has a play button (`#play`) that steps the clock through the active plan in 15-minute steps. 24-hour option `plan.dayHard`: routes at or above onsight only in daylight, weighted up by day.
- Zone map (`data-src/aspects.py` ZONE_OF): the 24 zones are the 24 red-labelled walls on "The Full Horseshoe" map, last page of the 2024 rules PDF (The Park, Carrion Cube and Goat Cave are not zones). North Forty is split into four sub-areas by comp zone (`NF_SPLIT`, estimated positions). End routes (`SPECIALS`, route field `sp` = 'W'/'E'): Full Horseshoe needs one lap on any west-end route (#10, 14, 24, 33, 36) and any east-end route (#641, 642, 648, 650, 651).
- Quick optimizer testing without a browser: load `docs/sun.js` and `docs/plan.js` in Node with a stub ctx; `Planner(...)._opt(plan)` and `._tl(plan)`.
